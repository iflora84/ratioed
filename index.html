Ratioed – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-16

Ratioed is a free word game with an optional one-time purchase that unlocks every chapter.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, streaks, daily puzzle results, energy count and whether you bought "Unlock everything" are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

iOS: no ads, no tracking

The App Store version contains no advertising and no ad SDK. It does not track you, does not use your advertising identifier, and collects no data at all.

Android: advertising

The Google Play version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information. Where required by law you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in your device's settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Buying "Unlock everything" stops all ads. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store tells the app only whether "Unlock everything" is owned.

Sharing

When you share a result, the app hands the text and image to your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere until you pick an app there, and we never see what you share or where.

Speech

Words are read aloud by your device's own text-to-speech. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not run our own servers, accounts, analytics or crash reporting, and we do not sell or share any data beyond what the advertising SDK described above collects.

Children

Ratioed is rated 12+ / Teen. Its slang is kept PG-13, but the app is not directed at children under 13; the Android version requests only general-audience ads.
